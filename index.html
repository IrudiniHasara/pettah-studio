Privacy policy

What we collect, why we collect it and the choices you have.

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Who we are

Pettah Studio (“we”, “us”) provides game, software and app development and 3D design services. You can reach us at hello@pettahstudio.com.

What we collect

  • Information you send us, such as your name, email address and project details, when you contact us.
  • Information you share while we work together, such as briefs, files and feedback.

This website doesn’t use advertising trackers or sell data. If we add analytics, we will update this page.

How we use it

We use your information to reply to you, prepare quotes, deliver the work you hire us for and manage our business records. We don’t use it for anything unrelated.

Sharing

We don’t sell your information. We share it only with trusted providers we need to run the studio, such as email, file storage and payment services, or when the law requires it.

Storage and security

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above or to meet legal obligations, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect it. No online service is completely secure, so we can’t guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing us. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

Our services are for businesses and adults. We don’t knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
